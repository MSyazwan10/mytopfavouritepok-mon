My Favorite Pokémon Across Regions

A simple showcase of standout Pokémon from Kanto, Johto, and Hoenn.

Featured Regions Overview

Here are the three regions and Pokémon highlighted on this page:

  • Kanto (Generation I): Charizard - Fire / Flying Type
  • Johto (Generation II): Tyranitar - Rock / Dark Type
  • Hoenn (Generation III): Rayquaza - Dragon / Flying Type

Favorite Pokémon Details

1. Charizard (Kanto Region)

Charizard is my all-time favorite Pokémon from the original Kanto region. It has high speed, powerful fire-based attacks, and an iconic design that inspired many trainers.

2. Tyranitar (Johto Region)

Tyranitar is a pseudo-legendary Pokémon introduced in Generation II. Its thick rock-like armor makes it nearly impervious to damage, and it can summon sandstorms in battle.

3. Rayquaza (Hoenn Region)

Rayquaza is a legendary Dragon and Flying type that lives high in the ozone layer. It is the trio master of Kyogre and Groudon, capable of calming massive weather disasters.
